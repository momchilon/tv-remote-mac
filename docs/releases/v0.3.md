# TV Remote for Mac v0.3

A full Samsung remote when you need it, the same compact menu-bar remote when you don't.

## What's new

- **Full / Compact:** expand the small remote into a wider, carefully arranged native panel and collapse it again. No auto-opening, hover activation or Dock icon.
- **Physical-remote layout:** a circular direction pad, separate volume and channel rockers, a 0–9 keypad with previous channel, and labelled red/green/yellow/blue A/B/C/D keys.
- **Playback and TV shortcuts:** rewind, play, pause, stop, fast-forward, record, TV menu, channel list, information and subtitles.
- **More controls:** search grouped controls by name or `KEY_…` code. Includes TV menus, direct inputs, picture/sound, captions, teletext, timers, Ambient, Multi-view and PiP.
- **114 distinct mapped commands:** 113 ordinary keys plus the existing confirmed power action. One shared command type connects the UI to the transport, with tests checking complete catalogue coverage.
- **Keyboard:** number row/numpad for channel digits and Page Up/Down for channel changes. Typing into search or settings does not send TV commands.
- **UI care:** separate button shapes, readable labels, colour-independent A/B/C/D identification, hover/press feedback, reduced-motion support, offline disabling, long-name truncation and scrolling for shorter displays.
- **Lean catalogue rendering:** fixed control groups are prepared once, button metadata uses direct lookup, and searching keeps a stable scroll view instead of rebuilding its identity on every edit. Everyday and extended controls share labels and icons.

TV discovery, multiple named remotes and existing per-TV pairing are retained. Use **Find TVs** in settings or enter an IP manually; switch saved remotes from the TV-name menu.

## Download and install

Download **TV-Remote-v0.3-arm64.dmg**, open it and drag **Notch Remote.app** into Applications. Quit an existing copy before replacing it. Open the app and click its remote-shaped menu-bar icon. Use **Full** for the new layout and **More controls** for the catalogue.

The download is for **Apple silicon Macs**, with a minimum deployment target of **macOS 13**. It is **ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized**; macOS may require manual approval in Privacy & Security. `SHA256SUMS` is provided to verify the download.

The original app name, bundle identifier and pairing location are unchanged. No personal TV settings, credentials or private device information are included in the download.

## Compatibility and limits

- **Samsung only.** Requests for [any TV brand or model](https://github.com/momchilon/tv-remote-mac/issues/new?template=tv-support.yml) remain welcome; support is not guaranteed.
- These are known Samsung wire mappings, **not 114 universally supported Tizen features**. Extended and legacy commands depend on the model, input and active app; unsupported keys can do nothing.
- A successful WebSocket write is not proof the TV performed the requested action. The new catalogue was exercised against a loopback test server, not by sending every key to a physical TV.
- No factory/service commands, unknown extension codes or power-confirmation bypass. The existing pinned TLS connection and offline gating are retained.
- No microphone/voice streaming, app-ID launcher, fetched channel directory, screen mirroring or cloud control.
- Discovery and wake retain their network/model limitations. Wake is best-effort and requires a known valid MAC and working standby networking.
- The Full/Compact preference lasts for the current app session, not across restarts.

## Verification

Release checks cover headless app/model tests, the complete key catalogue and JSON framing, all 113 non-power keys through the real loopback TLS/WebSocket transport, storage/discovery/trust tests, offscreen native UI previews, and bundle/signature/DMG validation. Desktop-click automation remains separately opt-in and was not used for these previews.
